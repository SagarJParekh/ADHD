Week 01 · Foundation
Observe & Capture
"Stop fighting your chaos. Start mapping it."
🔍 Awareness
Build your One Capture System System
Pick ONE place for everything — app or notebook. Every thought, task, worry goes here immediately, no organising yet.
→ Notion, Apple Notes, Google Keep, or pocket notebook. Rule: if it's in your head, it goes in the inbox.
Set up 3 daily Energy Log alarms Brain
Track energy, focus, mood (1–10) + what you just did/ate — 3x daily for 7 days. Use this app's Today Log.
→ Morning, afternoon, evening alarms. 60 seconds each. 7 days of data reveals your hidden patterns.
Install Screen Time tracker — observe only Brain
iOS Screen Time or Android Digital Wellbeing. No changes this week — just watch which apps pull you in and when.
→ Check weekly report on Day 7. No guilt. Just data. You act on this in Week 2.
Have the Partner ADHD Briefing conversation Relationship
What ADHD actually is (neuroscience, not excuses), what you're doing over 6 weeks, ask: "What's the one behaviour that affects you most?"
→ Listen without defending. Ask for patience, not management.

⚠ Pitfall

Wanting to do everything at once. Week 1 is awareness only. Boredom = correct. Give your ADHD brain data collection as the challenge.

✓ Win

7 days of energy logs + 20+ items in capture inbox. Nothing else required.
Week 02 · Structure
Time Anchors
"You don't need a schedule. You need 3 anchors."
⏰ Rhythm
Install Morning Launch Routine (20 min max) System
Before phone: Wake → Water → Cold water on face → Scan capture inbox (5 min) → Pick ONE task → Write on sticky note. Phone unlocked.
→ Same order every morning. Predictability trains your brain into launch state.
Install Midday Reset (5-min alarm) System
Midday alarm. Stop 5 minutes. Ask: Did I do my one task? What's pulling my attention? What do I need this afternoon?
→ Adjust afternoon based on answer. No guilt about the morning. Just recalibrate.
Install Night Shutdown Protocol (15 min) Brain
Review inbox → tomorrow's one task → 3 things that went okay → phone away → 10 min non-screen.
→ Signals to your brain the day is done. Directly improves sleep quality.
Implement Phone Friction System Brain
Top 3 dopamine apps off home screen into folder "intentional use." ALL non-essential notifications off. App time limits set.
→ Charger out of bedroom. Making unconscious phone-grabbing physically harder.

⚠ Pitfall

Making anchors too long. 20 min morning max. 5 min midday. 15 min night. Boring and short beats elaborate and abandoned.

✓ Win

All 3 anchors on 4 of 7 days. Missed days don't reset you — return next day.
Week 03 · Productivity
Task Completion
"Finish one thing. Just one."
🎯 Focus
Start Pomodoro — ADHD Edition System
15 min work / 5 min movement break. ONE task per block. Key rule: switching = conscious decision, never a drift.
→ Any timer app works. Urgency drives the ADHD brain — the countdown creates artificial deadline dopamine.
Try Body Doubling at least 3x Brain
Partner sits nearby while both work independently. Or YouTube "study with me" / Focusmate app.
→ Social presence regulates attention with zero interaction. One of the most powerful non-medication ADHD tools.
Apply Task Decomposition Rule to inbox System
Every heavy task needs a next PHYSICAL action. "Do taxes" → "Open laptop and find last year's tax folder."
→ Rewrite at least 10 tasks this way. Removes the freeze-on-entry problem entirely.
First Weekly Inbox Review (Sunday, 20 min) System
Process every item: Do it, Delegate it, Delete it, Defer it (specific date). Inbox back to zero.
→ Your weekly mental reset. Anything deferred with no date = delete it.

⚠ Pitfall

Using Pomodoro to plan more tasks instead of doing them. The timer is for execution only. Planning = Morning Launch.

✓ Win

5 tasks fully completed this week. Size irrelevant. "Replied to that email" counts as much as "finished the report."
Week 04 · Neuroscience
Body as Medicine
"Exercise is the closest thing to medication without medication."
💪 Biology
Commit to Minimum Viable Movement (4x/week) Body
Raise heart rate 20 min, 4 days/week. Fast walk, dancing, YouTube HIIT, cycling. Right after Morning Launch.
→ Type doesn't matter. The dopamine release is the goal. Fuels your most productive hours.
Upgrade Cold Water to On-Demand Reset Body
Anytime overwhelmed, impulsive, or emotionally flooded: 10–15 sec cold water on face or wrists. Use at least once in a tense moment.
→ Triggers vagus nerve. Reduces impulsivity within 30 seconds. Genuinely.
Set fixed wake time — same every day Body
Including weekends. Target 7.5 hours in bed. Keep room cool and dark. ADHD brain needs more sleep to restore dopamine sensitivity.
→ This single change stabilises your entire energy pattern more than almost anything else.
Switch to Protein-First mornings Body
Within 30 min of waking: 20g+ protein. Eggs, Greek yogurt, paneer, nuts + cheese. Avoids 2-hour dopamine crash from high-carb breakfasts.
→ Not a full meal. Just enough to stabilise neurotransmitter production.

⚠ Pitfall

All-or-nothing thinking. "Only 12 minutes" — go for 12. "Missed Monday" — go Tuesday. Never zero.

✓ Win

Moved 3+ days this week. Notice — even slightly — better focus on exercise days vs rest days.
Week 05 · External World
Relationships & Finances
"ADHD doesn't just affect you — it affects everyone around you."
💑 Systems
Have the Relationship Repair Conversation Relationship
Ask partner: "Top 3 ways my ADHD affects our relationship?" For each, propose a system fix. Us vs ADHD, not you vs me.
→ Example: "I'll add every important date to capture inbox immediately while you're saying it." Agree on weekly check-in.
Set up Weekly Partner Accountability Check-In Relationship
Every Sunday, 10 min max: share one win + one commitment. Partner listens and acknowledges — no advice, no reminders.
→ Schedule it at a fixed time right now. Makes accountability feel safe, not shameful.
Finance Automation Session (1 hour, permanent) Finance
Auto-pay ALL recurring bills. Auto-transfer savings on payday. Create "financial decisions" note reviewed weekly only.
→ Remove your brain from the financial loop entirely. One session, permanent fix.
Set up Shared Calendar with Partner System
Google Calendar shared. Every commitment goes in immediately when agreed. 24hr + 2hr reminders for anything important.
→ Add 5+ upcoming events right now to make it useful immediately.

⚠ Pitfall

Turning the relationship conversation into a blame session. You're co-designing systems, not apologising for existing.

✓ Win

All bills on auto-pay. Relationship conversation done. Shared calendar has 5+ upcoming items.
Week 06 · Mastery
Integrate & Sustain
"Build for the bad days, not the good ones."
🔄 OS
Write your One-Page ADHD Operating System System
3 anchor sequences + times, movement commitment, capture system, weekly review day, partner check-in, top 3 triggers, 3 reset tools.
→ Print it. Desk, fridge, bathroom mirror. Or phone wallpaper. Bad days: return to this page.
Design and memorise your Crash Protocol Brain
Pre-designed response for bad ADHD days. No decisions needed when you're least equipped to decide.
→ Protocol: (1) Cold water. (2) Smallest inbox item. (3) Walk 15 min, no phone. (4) Tell partner "rough day." That's it.
Do your 6-Week Energy Pattern Review Brain
Review all logs (use Charts tab). Find 5 best + 5 worst focus days. What preceded each? Sleep? Exercise? Food? Social?
→ Write your top 3 high-performance conditions. These are now non-negotiables to protect.
Design your Month 2 Challenge System
Deep work sessions, physical goal (5K), high-interest skill, or specific financial target. Write it with a 4-week target.
→ Pick whatever excites you most. Excitement = dopamine = sustained effort. Ambition on top of structure now.

⚠ Pitfall

Abandoning the system because "it's working now." ADHD doesn't disappear when symptoms reduce. The system IS the treatment.

✓ Win

Written one-page OS. Can name crash protocol from memory. Feel — even slightly — more in control than 6 weeks ago.

Crash Protocol
You're having a crash day. That's okay. Follow these steps.
Crash days are part of the system — not failures of it. The protocol already accounts for this.
01
Cold Water on Face
10–15 seconds. Wrists or face. Triggers vagus nerve. Reduces impulsivity within 30 seconds. Do this first, before anything else.
02
One Tiny Inbox Item
Open your capture inbox. Pick the smallest, easiest item. Do only that one thing. This closes one open loop and provides a real dopamine hit.
03
Walk 15 Minutes Outside
No phone. No podcast. Just walk. Sunlight + movement resets cortisol and dopamine simultaneously. This is not optional — it works.
04
Tell Partner "Rough Day"
Two words. No explanation needed. This breaks isolation without requiring emotional energy you don't have. That's all.

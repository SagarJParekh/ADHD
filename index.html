Week 01 · Foundation
Observe & Capture
"Stop fighting your chaos. Start mapping it."
Build your One Capture System System
Pick ONE place for everything. Every thought, task, worry goes here immediately.
→ Notion, Apple Notes, Google Keep, or pocket notebook. If it's in your head, it goes in the inbox.
Set up 3 daily Energy Log alarms Brain
Track energy, focus, mood (1–10) 3x daily for 7 days. Use this app's Today Log.
→ Morning, afternoon, evening alarms. 60 seconds each. 7 days reveals hidden patterns.
Install Screen Time — observe only Brain
iOS Screen Time. No changes this week — just watch which apps pull you in and when.
→ Check weekly report on Day 7. No guilt. Just data. Act on this in Week 2.
Partner ADHD Briefing conversation Relationship
Explain what ADHD is. Tell them what you're doing over 6 weeks. Ask: "What's the one behaviour that affects you most?"
→ Listen without defending. Ask for patience, not management.

⚠ Pitfall

Doing everything at once. Week 1 is awareness only. Boredom = correct.

✓ Win

7 days of logs + 20+ items in capture inbox.
Week 02 · Structure
Time Anchors
"You don't need a schedule. You need 3 anchors."
Install Morning Launch (20 min max) System
Before phone: Wake → Water → Cold water on face → Scan inbox (5 min) → Pick ONE task → Sticky note. Phone unlocked.
→ Same order every morning. Predictability trains your brain into launch state.
Install Midday Reset (5-min alarm) System
Midday alarm. Stop 5 min. Did I do my task? What's pulling me? What do I need this afternoon?
→ Adjust afternoon based on answer. No guilt about the morning.
Install Night Shutdown (15 min) Brain
Review inbox → tomorrow's one task → 3 things that went okay → phone away → 10 min non-screen.
→ Signals brain the day is done. Directly improves sleep quality.
Implement Phone Friction System Brain
Top 3 dopamine apps → folder "intentional use." ALL non-essential notifications off. Charger out of bedroom.
→ Making unconscious phone-grabbing physically harder.

⚠ Pitfall

Making anchors too long. 20/5/15 minutes max. Boring and short beats elaborate and abandoned.

✓ Win

All 3 anchors on 4 of 7 days. Missed days don't reset you.
Week 03 · Productivity
Task Completion
"Finish one thing. Just one."
Start Pomodoro — ADHD Edition System
15 min work / 5 min movement break. ONE task per block. Switching = conscious decision, never a drift.
→ Any timer app. Urgency drives the ADHD brain — countdown creates artificial deadline dopamine.
Try Body Doubling at least 3x Brain
Partner sits nearby while both work independently. Or YouTube "study with me" / Focusmate app.
→ Social presence regulates attention with zero interaction needed.
Apply Task Decomposition Rule System
Every heavy task needs a next PHYSICAL action. "Do taxes" → "Open laptop, find last year's tax folder."
→ Rewrite at least 10 tasks in your inbox this way. Removes the freeze-on-entry problem.
First Weekly Inbox Review (Sunday 20 min) System
Every item: Do it, Delegate it, Delete it, or Defer it (specific date). Inbox back to zero.
→ Your weekly mental reset. No date = delete it.

⚠ Pitfall

Using Pomodoro to plan tasks. The timer is for execution only.

✓ Win

5 tasks fully completed this week. Size irrelevant.
Week 04 · Neuroscience
Body as Medicine
"Exercise is the closest thing to medication without medication."
Minimum Viable Movement (4x/week) Body
Raise heart rate 20 min, 4 days/week. Fast walk, dancing, YouTube HIIT, cycling. Right after Morning Launch.
→ Type doesn't matter. Dopamine release is the goal. Fuels your most productive hours.
Upgrade Cold Water to On-Demand Reset Body
Anytime overwhelmed or impulsive: 10–15 sec cold water on face or wrists. Use in at least one tense moment.
→ Triggers vagus nerve. Reduces impulsivity within 30 seconds. Genuinely.
Set fixed wake time — same every day Body
Including weekends. Target 7.5 hours in bed. ADHD brain needs more sleep to restore dopamine sensitivity.
→ This single change stabilises your entire energy pattern more than almost anything else.
Switch to Protein-First mornings Body
Within 30 min of waking: 20g+ protein. Eggs, Greek yogurt, paneer, nuts + cheese.
→ Avoids the 2-hour dopamine crash from high-carb breakfasts.

⚠ Pitfall

All-or-nothing. "Only 12 minutes" — go for 12. Never zero.

✓ Win

Moved 3+ days this week. Notice better focus on those days.
Week 05 · External World
Relationships & Finances
"ADHD doesn't just affect you — it affects everyone around you."
Relationship Repair Conversation Relationship
Ask partner: "Top 3 ways my ADHD affects our relationship?" For each, propose one system fix. Us vs ADHD.
→ "I'll add every important date to my inbox immediately while you're saying it." Agree on weekly check-in.
Set up Weekly Partner Check-In Relationship
Every Sunday, 10 min: one win + one commitment. Partner listens and acknowledges — no advice.
→ Schedule it now at a fixed time. Makes accountability feel safe, not shameful.
Finance Automation Session (1 hour) Finance
Auto-pay ALL recurring bills. Auto-transfer savings on payday. Create "financial decisions" note reviewed weekly only.
→ Remove your brain from the financial loop. One session, permanent fix.
Set up Shared Calendar with Partner System
Google Calendar shared. Every commitment goes in immediately when agreed. 24hr + 2hr reminders.
→ Add 5+ upcoming events right now to make it useful immediately.

⚠ Pitfall

Turning the conversation into blame. Co-design systems. Us vs ADHD, always.

✓ Win

All bills on auto-pay. Conversation done. Shared calendar has 5+ items.
Week 06 · Mastery
Integrate & Sustain
"Build for the bad days, not the good ones."
Write your One-Page ADHD OS System
3 anchor sequences + times, movement, capture location, review day, partner check-in, top 3 triggers, 3 reset tools.
→ Print it. Put it where you see it daily. Bad days: return to this page.
Design and memorise your Crash Protocol Brain
Pre-designed response for bad ADHD days so you don't have to decide when least equipped.
→ (1) Cold water. (2) Smallest inbox item. (3) Walk 15 min, no phone. (4) Tell partner "rough day."
Do your 6-Week Energy Pattern Review Brain
Go to Charts tab. Find 5 best + 5 worst focus days. What preceded each? Sleep? Exercise? Food?
→ Write your top 3 high-performance conditions. These become non-negotiables.
Design your Month 2 Challenge System
Deep work, 5K, high-interest skill, or financial target. Write it with a specific 4-week target date.
→ Pick whatever excites you most. Excitement = dopamine = sustained effort.

⚠ Pitfall

Abandoning the system because "it's working now." The system IS the treatment. Keep the anchors.

✓ Win

Written OS. Crash protocol memorised. Feel even slightly more in control.

One Commitment for Next Week
You're having a crash day. That's okay.
Crash days are part of the system — not failures of it. Follow these four steps.
01
Cold Water on Face
10–15 seconds. Wrists or face. Triggers vagus nerve. Reduces impulsivity within 30 seconds. Do this first.
02
One Tiny Inbox Item
Open your capture inbox. Pick the smallest, easiest item. Do only that one thing. Closes one open loop — real dopamine hit.
03
Walk 15 Minutes Outside
No phone. No podcast. Just walk. Sunlight + movement resets cortisol and dopamine simultaneously.
04
Tell Partner "Rough Day"
Two words. No explanation needed. Breaks isolation without requiring emotional energy you don't have.
After the Crash
🚫 Don't catastrophise — one bad day doesn't break the system.
↩️ Just return — pick up your next anchor. That's all.
📊 Log the crash — data not shame. What triggered it?
